Privacy Policy for Mausam Nepal

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

Introduction

Mausam Nepal is a weather information application that provides real-time weather data and forecasts for locations within Nepal. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Data Sources

Mausam Nepal collects and displays weather information from the following sources:

  • Nepal Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) - Primary data source for weather information, forecasts, and meteorological bulletins
  • OpenWeatherMap - Supplementary weather data service

Language and Content

  • The app supports both English and Nepali languages
  • Some features are available only in Nepali due to direct extraction of data from government websites in Nepali language
  • We display the data as provided by the source websites without translation to maintain accuracy

Location Services

  • The app is designed to work exclusively for locations within Nepal
  • Even if you are outside Nepal, you will need to select a Nepali location to access weather information
  • Location data is used only to provide relevant weather information and is not stored or shared

Information We Collect

Mausam Nepal does not collect, store, or share any personal information about its users.

We only:

  • Access your location (with your permission) to show weather for your area
  • Display publicly available weather data from government and third-party sources

Features Provided

The app provides the following weather information:

  • Current weather conditions for your location
  • 3-hour forecast
  • 3-day forecast
  • Major cities forecast
  • Latest bulletins including:
    • Cold wave monitoring
    • Monsoon monitoring
    • High altitude forecast
    • 24-hour forecast bulletin
    • 3-day forecast bulletin
    • Weekly weather outlook

Visual Experience

  • App themes automatically change based on current weather conditions (sunny, rainy, cloudy, night, etc.)
  • This personalization is done locally on your device and no data is collected

Third-Party Services

We use OpenWeatherMap API to supplement weather data. Please refer to OpenWeatherMap's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal data at risk. All weather data displayed is publicly available information from government sources.
