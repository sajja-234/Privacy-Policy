Privacy Policy for Balanzy

Last Updated: February 1, 2026

Introduction

Balanzy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Balanzy (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully.

Important: By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for and use Balanzy, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)

Financial Information

  • Expense details (title, amount, date, category, notes)
  • Split arrangements (how expenses are divided among group members)
  • Payment status information
  • Payment method details (stored securely in our database)
  • Balance information (who owes whom)

Group and Social Information

  • Group names and descriptions
  • Group membership information
  • Invite codes for groups
  • Notification preferences

Technical Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App usage data and analytics
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • IP address and device identifiers

Camera and Scanning Information

  • QR code scans (processed locally, not stored)
  • Receipt images (if you choose to upload them)

Location and Connectivity

  • Local network connectivity status (for peer-to-peer sharing)
  • Bluetooth connectivity status (for nearby device discovery)
  • NFC interactions (for contactless sharing)

How We Collect Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  1. Direct Input: Information you provide when creating an account, adding expenses, creating groups, or updating your profile.
  2. Automatic Collection: Technical information collected automatically through Firebase Analytics and our App's functionality.
  3. Third-Party Services: Information from Google Sign-In when you choose to authenticate using your Google account.
  4. Peer-to-Peer: Information shared when you use QR codes, NFC, or local network features to connect with other users.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Account Management

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Process sign-in requests

Expense Tracking

  • Record and manage shared expenses
  • Calculate splits and balances
  • Track payment status
  • Generate expense reports and analytics

Group Functionality

  • Create and manage groups
  • Process group invitations
  • Manage group membership
  • Facilitate group expense sharing

Communication

  • Send push notifications about new expenses, payment requests, and group activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important updates about the App

Analytics and Improvement

  • Analyze app usage patterns
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Develop new features

Security

  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Enforce our terms of service
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

We use Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) to store and process your data:

  • Authentication data is stored in Firebase Authentication
  • User profiles, groups, expenses, and payment methods are stored in Firebase Firestore
  • Receipt images (if uploaded) are stored in Firebase Storage
  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Data at rest is encrypted using Google Cloud Platform's security measures
Security Notice: We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

With Other Users

  • Your display name, email, and profile photo are visible to members of groups you join
  • Expense details are shared with other group members
  • Balance information is shared with relevant group members

With Service Providers

  • Firebase/Google Cloud Platform (data hosting and authentication)
  • Google Sign-In (if you choose to use Google authentication)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, subpoenas).
